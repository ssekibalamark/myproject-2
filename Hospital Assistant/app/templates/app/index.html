Hospital Assistant

A clinical support system where AI and healthcare professionals work side by side. Hospital Assistant helps doctors and specialists diagnose faster, suggest lab tests and medications, and manage patient records — with every AI recommendation reviewed and approved by a qualified specialist before it reaches the patient.

AI-Assisted Diagnosis

Symptoms, lab results, and doctor's notes are analyzed to suggest possible diagnoses, required lab tests, and medications — always reviewed by a specialist first.

Patient-Centered Records

Complete patient profiles including next of kin, encounters, and care history, kept securely in the hospital database.

Role-Based Access

Staff registration is managed by the ICT department, with each medical role granted access only to the tools relevant to their work.
